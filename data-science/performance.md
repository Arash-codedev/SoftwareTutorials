## Performance Terminologies

- **Underfitting**
    - Easy to detect.

- **Overfitting**
    - Hard to detect.
    - **Cross-validation:**
        - K-fold cross-validation (useful for small datasets).
        - Train-Validation-Test Split (useful for larger datasets).
    - **Prevention:**
        - Data augmentation (e.g., adding noise).
        - Ensemble methods.
        - Early stopping.
        - L1 Regularization (Lasso Regression).
        - L2 Regularization (Ridge Regression).

## Preventing Bad Data

- Cross-check data.
- Remove outliers when appropriate.
- Avoid outdated data.
