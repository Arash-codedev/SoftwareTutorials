# Data Science Statistics

## Descriptive Analytics (Summarizing Data)
- **Measures of Central Tendency:** Mean, median, mode
- **Measures of Dispersion:** Variance, standard deviation, interquartile range (IQR), range
- **Data Distribution Features:** Skewness, kurtosis, percentiles, quartiles

## Hypothesis Testing & Experimentation
- **Formulating Hypotheses:**
    - $H_0$: Null hypothesis (status quo / no effect)
    - $H_1$: Alternative hypothesis (what you're trying to prove)
    - *Which one is more consistent with data?*

- **Parametric Hypothesis Testing (Normally Distributed):**
    - **Z-tests:** How far the sample mean is from the population mean.
    - **t-tests:** Tests whether the means of one or two groups differ significantly.
    - **ANOVA (Analysis of Variance):** Tests whether the means of 3+ groups differ significantly.

- **Non-Parametric Hypothesis Testing (Non-Normal Data):**
    - Chi-square tests
    - Mann-Whitney U test
    - Kruskal-Wallis H test
    - Wilcoxon tests (Signed-Rank / Rank-Sum)

- **Estimation & Uncertainty:** Confidence intervals, margin of error, point estimates, statistical power

- **Significance Metrics** (Knowing if results are real or just due to chance):
    - **P-values:** Comparing observed test statistics against theoretical probability distributions (e.g., the normal distribution).
    - **Significance Level:** Alpha level ($\alpha$)

## Probability & Predictive Modeling
- **Probability Theory:** Probability distributions (Normal, Binomial, Poisson), random variables, Bayes' theorem
- **Relationships & Regression:** Correlation coefficients, covariance, linear regression, logistic regression
